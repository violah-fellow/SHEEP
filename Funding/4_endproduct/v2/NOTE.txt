v2 changes from v1 (v1 prompt taken from patents v3):

- Reframed output from a single top-2-ranked pick to independent TRUE/FALSE per category,
  matching the rescat pipeline's multi-label schema — grants can have more than one end
  product type, unlike the patents pipeline this prompt was originally written for.
- Removed Cross-cutting entirely. Its two former uses are now handled differently:
    - "grant spans multiple named product types" -> flag every applicable category True
      (multi-label already expresses this; no separate label needed).
    - "food-ingredient COMPOSITION with no specific product framing" (textured proteins,
      enzyme preparations, encapsulated ingredients) -> merged into Agnostic. This is a
      real behaviour change worth reviewing: these grants previously got a distinct label
      from equipment/strain/process grants (which were Agnostic); now both fall under
      Agnostic since there's no other bucket for "food-related but no specific product"
      once Cross-cutting is gone.
- Added a proper definition for Infant formula. In v1 it was only named inside the Dairy
  key-decision-rule bullet but had no entry in the "End Product Categories" list at the
  bottom — a real gap (v1 said "12 categories" but only defined 11). v2 now defines all 12.
- Simplified the Dairy rules: v1 routed "spans multiple dairy subtypes" and "dairy alongside
  another AP product type" to Cross-cutting. Since multi-label is now possible, v2 instead
  says to flag each specific dairy subcategory that clearly applies, and to flag Dairy
  alongside Meat/Fish and seafood/Eggs/etc. when a grant genuinely spans both. The general
  Dairy fallback is now reserved strictly for genuine ambiguity about which single dairy
  subtype applies, not for multi-subtype or multi-product grants.
- Kept the comprehensive specificity guardrails as-is (don't infer from physical form, don't
  infer end product from external/common-use knowledge, paste/purée is not automatically a
  spread) since these are still needed regardless of single- vs multi-label output.

Downstream (in prompt_endproduct_testing.ipynb): the 5 dairy booleans (Milk and milk
proteins, Yoghurt and fermented dairy, Cheese, Cream and ice cream, Infant formula) plus the
general Dairy boolean are collapsed into the ground truth's two-column format — any of them
True sets "End product type" to include "Dairy", and the specific true subcategory(ies)
(excluding the general Dairy flag) are written to "sub-end product".
