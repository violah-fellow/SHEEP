Notes on v1 performance:
Overall, performed surprisingly well! A few points where it's missing the mark but the majority of failures I actually just agreed with the LLM so all good here.

Ingredient optimisation is being over-assigned — the LLM is assigning it whenever ingredients are mentioned, even when the grant is purely about finished product development. Ocean Bite, Tackling barriers (fat/oleogels), STARS burger, and the plant-based extrusion grant all have this problem. The distinction needed is that Ingredient optimisation requires the ingredient itself to be the object of study, not just an input to a product.
Consumer & market research is being over-assigned — sensory evaluation during product development is being categorised as consumer research. The fat/oleogels grant and SUSWEED both have this issue. Your note about human taste testing is the right fix.
Food safety is being over-assigned for starter culture selection — VegVit's safety screening of starter cultures is operational safety testing within a fermentation process, not food safety research. This should be Strain development.
Strain development is being under-assigned — VegVit and FAVORIT both involve selecting and screening bacterial cultures, which should trigger Strain development, but the LLM isn't picking it up.
Texturization vs Ingredient optimisation vs End product formulation boundaries are blurry — the extrusion grant should be Texturization only, but the LLM is splitting across all three because extrusion sits at the intersection of all of them.
Plant waste side streams = ingredient optimisation
Recombinant protein production in crops – is that crop development? Strain development?
Cross-cutting is unnecessary and was largely there to cut time when doing manually to avoid writing like 4+ categories.
Human taste testing as part of product development is just end product formulation and not consumer & market research.
