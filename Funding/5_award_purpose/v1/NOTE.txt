v1 prompt written from scratch (unlike end product/rescat, there was no existing patents/pubs
prompt to adapt for award purpose).

Basis: reviewed sample titles/abstracts for each of the 5 atomic "Award purpose" values found in
Funding2026_inscope.xlsx (Research and development, Education and training, Networking, Equipment
and infrastructure, Research infrastructure), then wrote category definitions and decision rules
directly from those patterns. Multi-label TRUE/FALSE framing throughout, matching the rescat and
end-product prompts, since the ground truth itself has real multi-label combos (e.g. 81 rows are
"Research and development, Education and training").

Judgment call worth reviewing on your first pass: Research infrastructure (only 4 ground-truth
rows, the rarest label) and Equipment and infrastructure (28 rows) are the two most confusable
categories — both fund physical capacity. The line drawn here: Research infrastructure = shared,
institutional, or multi-partner research facility/hub/instrumentation network supporting future
research broadly (e.g. NAPIC, a national alt-protein centre); Equipment and infrastructure = a
single organisation's own physical production/manufacturing equipment or facility, aimed at
making/scaling product rather than general research capacity. Confirmed with the user before
writing the prompt this way, but it's based on only 4 real examples of the rarer label, so worth
extra scrutiny during review.

Research and development is treated as the default category (present in 1603 of 1675 labelled
rows) — the prompt tells the model to flag it True for the large majority of grants and only
withhold it when a grant exclusively funds one of the other four purposes with no R&D activity of
its own described.

Trimmed shortly after first draft: the initial version had a "Key decision rules" bullet per
category that mostly just restated its own category definition in different words, plus a
multi-label rules bullet duplicating the intro paragraph. Unlike end product, these 5 categories
are mostly non-overlapping domains with only one genuine cross-category ambiguity (Equipment vs
Research infrastructure), so "Key decision rules" was cut down to just that one bullet, and the
category definitions list is now the single source of truth for scope/examples/exceptions.

Known limitation, investigated and NOT fixed (v1 results review): the LLM sometimes omits
"Education and training" for grants that also have "Research and development" true. Reviewing
awardpurpose_v1_claude-sonnet-4-6_results.xlsx, 9 of 10 rows where ground truth has "Education and
training" but the LLM didn't flag it were university-affiliated PIs (Reading, Oxford, King's
College London, Copenhagen, York, Wageningen, Nottingham) — a strong-looking signal that suggested
a PI-organisation-based override (force "Education and training" true whenever the PI is a
university/college/institute/association).

That override was checked against the FULL 1678-row ground truth before building it, and rejected:
the two conditional probabilities point in different directions. Within the missed rows, 90% were
university-affiliated — but among ALL university-affiliated grants in the dataset, only 15.2% have
"Education and training" in ground truth (vs a 6.9% dataset-wide baseline), so forcing the label
for every University PI would be wrong 84.8% of the time. The other name patterns tested are worse:
"college" wrong 64.3% of the time, "institute" 96.9%, "association" 100% (n=15 — includes dedicated
applied-research bodies like RISE and IRTA that do pure R&D with no training component, and the
Danish Vegetarian Association, an advocacy body, not a research/training institution). University
PIs ARE enriched for Education and training (~2.2x baseline) but nowhere near deterministically
enough to automate.

Conclusion: this is an accepted limitation, not a prompt or pipeline bug. It likely reflects the
original ground-truth curators using information not present in the title/abstract at all (e.g. an
attached PhD studentship) — the same category of gap as the Cocoa/Fats/Snacks/Sugar vocabulary
mismatch already documented for end product (4_endproduct/v2/NOTE.txt). No text-based approach —
LLM prompt wording or a PI-organisation-name heuristic — can close a gap caused by evidence that
isn't in the classified text. Don't re-propose a PI-organisation override without re-deriving these
numbers first.
