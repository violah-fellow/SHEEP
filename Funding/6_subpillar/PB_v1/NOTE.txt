v1 written from scratch (no existing patents/pubs prompt covers Plant-based subpillar — the ported
patents prompt only ever covered Fermentation-pillar BF/PF/NA).

Basis: pulled 14 real ground-truth examples of "TF" (Traditional fermentation) grants before
writing this. Overwhelmingly classic/artisanal microbial fermentation techniques (lactic acid
fermentation of legumes/pulses/cereals, solid-state fungal fermentation, kefir/yoghurt-style
cultures) applied as a processing step to plant protein raw materials — either to remove
anti-nutritional factors and improve digestibility/texture/flavour, or to create plant-based
analogues of fermented dairy/meat products (fermented vegan cheese, plant-based yoghurt,
tempeh-like hybrids). Very few titles literally say "tempeh"/"miso"/"koji" by name, but the
technique matches; a notable number of examples had missing/NaN abstracts, so the title alone
often has to carry the signal.

Only 75 of 1027 Plant-based rows (7%) have TF in ground truth — the other 93% are blank, which is
the normal/expected outcome, not a failure to classify. The prompt is written to make this explicit
("flagging True is the exception, not the default") so the model doesn't over-trigger on any
mention of fermentation-adjacent vocabulary in an otherwise unrelated Plant-based grant.

A small number of Plant-based rows (5 total) have anomalous non-TF Sub-production pillar values
(CellAg, BF, PF, Algae x2) — these read as algae-related mislabeling noise from elsewhere in the
taxonomy, not a real second Plant-based subpillar scheme, and are excluded from the notebook's
sampling/scoring for this pillar rather than folded into the prompt.

Key distinction worth remembering on review: this prompt is about fermentation as a *processing
technique* used within a Plant-based grant, not about the grant's underlying production platform.
A grant where microbial/fungal fermentation IS the production platform (i.e. genuinely belongs to
the Fermentation pillar, not Plant-based) is out of scope here entirely — that split happens
upstream of this prompt, at the Production platform level.
