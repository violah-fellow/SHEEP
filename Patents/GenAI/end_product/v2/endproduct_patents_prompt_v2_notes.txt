1. PHYSICAL FORMAT INFERENCE — EXTEND ANTI-INFERENCE RULE

Rows 36 and 68 show the LLM inferring product category from the ingredient's form:
- Row 36: Edible beads with fruit filling → LLM inferred Chocolate/confectionery.
  GT: Cross-cutting. Viola: "doesn't mention the dessert specifically."
  Encapsulated ingredients or delivery systems (beads, spheres, microcapsules) with a
  flavoured filling are not automatically confectionery — no specific end product stated
  → Cross-cutting.
- Row 68: Vegetable paste from Lactobacillus fermentation → LLM inferred Spreads
  ("paste" = condiment). GT: Cross-cutting. Viola: "Cross-cutting since no specific end
  product mentioned."

Fix: Extended the IMPORTANT notice to explicitly prohibit inferring product category from
physical form (beads, microcapsules, gels, pastes, purées). Also added clarification to
the Spreads bullet and definition: "paste" or "purée" alone does not trigger Spreads.

---

2. SWEETENED CONDENSED MILK ANALOGUE → CHOCOLATE

Row 39: Dairy-free sweetened condensed milk analogue → LLM: Dairy, GT: Chocolate,
either=TRUE.
Viola: "specifically sweetened condensed milk which is a dessert/sweet."
Sweetened condensed milk is used as a confectionery ingredient, not as a direct dairy
replacement (it is sweet, not savoury). Its plant-based analogue belongs in Chocolate,
desserts, and confectionery.

Fix: Added explicit rule to Chocolate preamble bullet and Chocolate definition: dairy-free
analogues of sweetened condensed milk → Chocolate, desserts, and confectionery.

---

3. DAIRY + NON-DAIRY PRODUCT TYPES → CROSS-CUTTING

Row 54: Seaweed flour procedure for milk-based aliments (desserts, yogurts, ice cream,
whipping cream) AND meat/vegan analogues → LLM: Dairy, GT: Cross-cutting, either=TRUE.
Viola: "Cross-cutting since it also mentions the application in meat or vegan analogues."
The current prompt has the rule "multiple dairy types → Cross-cutting" but it did not
catch the case where dairy is mentioned alongside a non-dairy product type.

Fix: Added explicit preamble rule: "Assign Dairy only when the patent is exclusively
about dairy-analogue products. If the abstract also mentions other AP product types
(meat, fish, food categories generally) → Cross-cutting."

---

4. ENZYME COMPOSITION vs METHOD — CROSS-CUTTING vs AGNOSTIC BOUNDARY

Two error types pointing in opposite directions:

Row 71: PAD enzyme to obtain improved food — LLM: Cross-cutting, GT: Agnostic,
either=FALSE.
Viola: "Agnostic because of method focus and no end product." Abstract: "a method for
improving properties of a food product containing a protein" — food product is generic.
This is a METHOD patent (the process is the innovation, not a food ingredient).
→ Too generic to indicate any specific product type → Agnostic.

Rows 84 and 100: Stabilized liquid deamidase COMPOSITIONS → LLM: Agnostic,
GT: Cross-cutting, either=TRUE.
Viola: "borderline but as it can be added to food I would say Cross-cutting."
These are COMPOSITION patents (the enzyme formulation is the innovation). Even though
no specific food product is mentioned in the abstract, enzyme compositions formulated
for food use (deamidases, transglutaminases, proteases) are food ingredients → Cross-cutting.

Fix: Clarified the composition vs method distinction in both the Cross-cutting bullet
and definition, and in the Agnostic definition:
  - Enzyme COMPOSITION patents (where the formulation is the claimed innovation)
    → Cross-cutting even if no food product is mentioned in the abstract.
  - METHOD patents applying an enzyme or process to modify generic "food products" or
    "food products containing protein" without naming any specific product type → Agnostic.

5. PROCESSING EQUIPMENT WITH NAMED PRODUCT → ASSIGN PRODUCT CATEGORY

Row 90: Milk foaming device for coffee-milk applications → LLM: Dairy, GT: Milk and
milk proteins, either=FALSE.
Viola: "specifically mentions milk not other dairy products so Milk and milk proteins."
The existing prompt sends equipment → Agnostic unless a food product is stated. This
device explicitly says "milk foam" and "milk modifications" for coffee/beverage use.
Equipment with an explicitly named product application should be assigned that product
category — Agnostic is reserved for equipment with NO named food product context.

Fix: Added exception to both the Agnostic preamble bullet and Agnostic definition:
"Processing equipment that explicitly names a specific product application (e.g. a milk
frothing device for milk/coffee applications, a cheese press) → assign the corresponding
product category, not Agnostic."
