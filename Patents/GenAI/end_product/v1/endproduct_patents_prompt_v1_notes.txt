3. LLM INFERRING END PRODUCT FROM EXTERNAL KNOWLEDGE — LARGEST ERROR CLASS

Multiple cases where the LLM assigned a specific category by inferring how an ingredient,
organism, or technology is commonly used — information NOT stated in the abstract.

Affected cases:
- Row 7: Agglomeration system → LLM said Milk ("commonly applied in dairy powder
  manufacturing"). Abstract describes processing equipment; no product mentioned. → Agnostic.
- Row 27: Oat-based seasoning → LLM said Milk (inferred oat milk from "oat suspension").
  Abstract says "oat-based flavourful seasoning / intermediate in manufacture of other food
  products." → Spreads (seasoning as a condiment/sauce ingredient).
- Row 32: Device for generating microtissue fragments → LLM said Meat ("applicable to
  cultivated meat"). Abstract describes a cutting device for mincing biological tissue;
  no food product is stated. → Agnostic.
- Row 79: Novel Carnobacterium maltaromaticum strain → LLM said Fish ("well-known for use
  in fish products"). Abstract says "preparation of a food product" — completely generic.
  → Agnostic.

Fix: Added a prominent IMPORTANT notice at the top of the preamble:
"Base your classification ONLY on what the title and abstract explicitly state about the
intended end product application. Do NOT infer an end product from external knowledge
about how an ingredient, organism, or technology is commonly used."

4. CROSS-CUTTING VS AGNOSTIC — CLARIFIED BOUNDARY

Several cases where Cross-cutting and Agnostic were confused, with Viola's corrections:

- Viola accepts Agnostic (LLM was right, GT wrong):
  Row 15: Pea + rapeseed protein isolate → Agnostic (GT was Eggs — wrong label)
  Row 58: Method for predicting raw material functionality → Agnostic (GT was Milk — wrong)
  Row 63: Bioreactor system for biomass production → Agnostic (GT was Fish — wrong)
  Row 67: Moringa oleifera microcapsules → Agnostic (GT was Cross-cutting — wrong)
  Row 82: Highly soluble pea starch as maltodextrin substitute → Agnostic (GT was Cheese)

- Viola says Cross-cutting (LLM was right, GT wrong):
  Row 83: Mycelium ingredients explicitly described for meat, fish, dairy, and beverages
    → Cross-cutting (GT was Agnostic — wrong; abstract names multiple product types)
  Row 84: Stabilized liquid deamidase compositions → Cross-cutting (food-use enzyme with
    broad applicability; GT was Yoghurt — wrong)
  Row 100: Method for stabilising liquid deamidase compositions → Cross-cutting (same)

- Viola says Cross-cutting (LLM was wrong, GT was wrong):
  Row 51: Textured wheat gluten composition → Cross-cutting. GT was Agnostic; LLM said Meat.
    The abstract says "its use" without specifying; textured protein = food ingredient without
    specific product targeting → Cross-cutting.

Fix: Rewrote the Cross-cutting and Agnostic definitions to clarify the boundary:
  - Cross-cutting: explicitly multi-product patents PLUS food ingredient compositions
    (textured proteins, food-use enzymes, encapsulated food ingredients) with no specific
    product targeting.
  - Agnostic: equipment, bioreactors, strain/biomass characterisation, extraction methods,
    bioproduction processes with no food product framing. Does NOT include food ingredient
    compositions — those go to Cross-cutting.

5. ALGAE / MICROALGAE — CLARIFICATION

Two identical patents (lacto-fermented algae) caused two separate errors (rows 47 and 72):
GT was "Eggs and egg proteins" (wrong label), LLM assigned Fish and seafood (wrong), and
Viola says Agnostic. Algae lacto-fermentation process producing "ready-to-eat algae in
vegetable form and a fermentation juice" — not an alternative protein seafood product.

Fix: Added explicit preamble rule: "algae or microalgae patents not explicitly described
as fish/seafood substitutes should NOT be assigned Fish and seafood." Added the same note
to the Fish and seafood definition.
