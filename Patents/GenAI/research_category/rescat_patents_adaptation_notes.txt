Research category prompts — publications → patents adaptation
Applied to: prompt_rescat_pubs_PB_v6.md, prompt_rescat_pubs_CM_v4.md, prompt_rescat_pubs_F_v6.md, prompt_rescat_pubs_CC_v4.md

---

1. LANGUAGE THROUGHOUT — ALL PROMPTS
   Every instance of "publication", "paper", "article", and "study" replaced with "patent".
   "novel contribution of the paper" / "primary research contribution of the paper" →
   "primary invention or claimed contribution of the patent".
   "A category should only be assigned if the paper actively investigates, develops, or measures" →
   "A category should only be assigned if the patent actively claims, develops, or describes an
   invention in that domain".
   Worked examples updated from "A paper doing X → Category" to "A patent claiming X → Category".

2. CATEGORIES REMOVED — ALL PROMPTS
   Consumer & market research: Patents do not study consumer behaviour, purchasing intent,
   product acceptance, or labelling perception. Removed entirely.
   Impact assessments: Patents do not conduct LCA, TEA, carbon footprinting, or systematic
   policy reviews. Removed entirely.
   Category counts updated accordingly:
     PB: 9 specific → 7 specific; 10 total → 8 total
     CM: 9 specific → 7 specific; 10 total → 8 total
     F:  11 specific → 9 specific; 12 total → 10 total
     CC: 8 specific → 7 specific (original count was already inconsistent); 9 total → 8 total

3. HEALTH & NUTRITION REFRAMED — ALL PROMPTS
   Publication definition: studies measuring physiological outcomes (population studies, in vivo/
   in vitro studies with a physiological primary endpoint).
   Patent definition: inventions specifically designed to improve nutritional composition,
   digestibility, or health-relevant properties — e.g. processes to reduce anti-nutritional
   factors (phytates, trypsin inhibitors), increase protein bioavailability, enhance micronutrient
   content, or achieve specific nutritional targets.
   Boundary with Ingredient Optimisation / End Product Formulation preserved: general nutritional
   characterisation or profiling as part of ingredient/product development remains in those
   categories; Health & nutrition requires the nutritional improvement to be the primary aim
   of the invention.

4. FOOD SAFETY & QUALITY REFRAMED — ALL PROMPTS
   Publication definition: safety assessments (microbial profiling, contaminant detection,
   allergenicity studies, assay validation).
   Patent definition: inventions for improving or ensuring safety and quality — novel preservation
   methods, antimicrobial treatments, allergen detection or reduction methods, novel analytical
   methods or assays. Shifted from "conducting assessments" to "claiming inventions that achieve
   or enable safety/quality outcomes".
   Boundaries retained: excludes process/occupational/environmental safety of manufacturing
   facilities and equipment maintenance.
   CM-specific: removed "analysis of novel food regulatory approval pathways and frameworks"
   (a publication-type activity) from the definition.

5. CROP DEVELOPMENT REFRAMED — PB ONLY
   Publication definition: "Breeding of crops and increased use of underutilised protein crops
   for higher protein yields, protein quality, and functionality."
   Patent definition: inventions for developing novel crop varieties or plant lines through
   breeding, genetic modification, or gene editing (e.g. CRISPR) aimed at improving protein
   yield, quality, or processability. Explicitly includes novel breeding methods and tools.
   Rationale: patents claim novel varieties and modification methods rather than reporting
   breeding research outcomes.

6. OTHER REFRAMED — ALL PROMPTS
   Publication Other included: broad review articles, opinions, perspectives, commentary,
   workshop reports, case studies, recipe development.
   Patent Other: none of these publication types exist for patents. Replaced with patent-
   appropriate catch-all examples: patents spanning multiple categories without a dominant
   focus; general manufacturing infrastructure patents; patents covering packaging, distribution,
   or storage; and inventions not covered by the named categories.
   Also removed: the sentence "Other should be assigned to broad review articles that span
   multiple of the defined categories" and the F-prompt paragraph on review/perspective
   handling ("Review articles and perspective pieces receive Other unless...").

7. CATEGORY DEFINITIONS (SHARED) — MINOR WORDING ADJUSTMENTS
   Ingredient optimisation, End product formulation, Texturization methods, Bioprocess design,
   Scaffolding, Strain development, Target molecule selection, Cell line development,
   Cell culture media, Feedstocks: definitions preserved structurally; "study", "paper", and
   "novel contribution" language updated to "invention", "patent", and "claimed contribution".
   The substantive boundaries between these categories (e.g. Ingredient Optimisation vs End
   Product Formulation; Feedstocks vs Bioprocess Design) are unchanged.
