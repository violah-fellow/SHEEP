1. TARGET MOLECULE SELECTION REGRESSIONS FROM V3 — F

v3 introduced "do not let the presence of a named target molecule override
[Bioprocess]" which was too broad. It correctly fixed the casein production method
cases (rows 4/9 in v2) but also caught two legitimate TMS cases:

- Row 5: "RECOMBINANT PRODUCTION OF A COLLAGEN PEPTIDE PREPARATION" — recombinant
  collagen is a target molecule; the production method is not the primary novelty.
  v3 prompt pulled this to Bioprocess incorrectly.
- Row 39: "Lipases, compositions, methods and uses thereof" — a wild-type microbial
  lipolytic enzyme with specific fatty acid chain-length selectivity. The enzyme's
  specific molecular properties are the primary claimed contribution → TMS.
  v3 prompt pulled this to Ingredient optimisation.

Training labels confirmed correct: Target molecule selection for both.

Fix (F only):
- Rewrote the Bioprocess preamble bullet to specify it applies when the PRIMARY
  NOVELTY IS THE PROCESS STEPS OR PROTOCOL, not merely the fact that a protein is
  produced recombinantly.
- Rewrote the TMS preamble bullet to explicitly include "characterising a novel enzyme
  or functional protein for its specific molecular properties → TMS."
- Expanded TMS definition to explicitly cover: (a) naturally-occurring/wild-type
  enzymes where molecular properties are the primary contribution (e.g. lipases,
  proteases), (b) recombinant expression of food proteins (e.g. collagen, caseins),
  and (c) the key rule: assign TMS when the target molecule or enzyme itself is the
  primary invention, regardless of whether a production method is also described.

2. END PRODUCT FORMULATION VS TEXTURIZATION — F (still unresolved, moved to preamble)

Rows 19 and 46 ("FOOD OR FEED PRODUCT" with alginate structuring; "COMPOSITION
COMPRISING TEXTURED FUNGAL PROTEIN PIECES") — both are product COMPOSITION patents
where texture is a property of the formula, not a separate process/apparatus invention.
The composition rule added to the EPF definition in v3 had no effect.

Fix (F only):
- Moved the composition rule to the preamble as an explicit bullet example, which
  carries more weight than definition text based on observed model behaviour:
  "A patent claiming a finished product COMPOSITION (where texture or structure is a
  property of the formula) → End product formulation (not Texturization methods)."
