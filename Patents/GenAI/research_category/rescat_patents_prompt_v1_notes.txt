Research category prompts — v1 test run optimisation notes
Applied to: prompt_rescat_patents_PB.md, prompt_rescat_patents_CM.md, prompt_rescat_patents_F.md, prompt_rescat_patents_CC.md

Test set sizes: PB n=40, CM n=42, F n=51, CC n=38
Primary correct before: PB 60%, CM 83%, F 67%, CC 68%
Either correct before: PB 75%, CM 91%, F 78%, CC 84%

---

1. STRAIN DEVELOPMENT MASSIVELY UNDERDETECTED — PB and F

The largest error class across PB and F. The LLM repeatedly classified patents for novel bacterial
or fungal strains as Ingredient optimisation (seeing the downstream effect: ingredient modification)
or Texturization methods (seeing the downstream purpose: e.g. exopolysaccharide texturisation)
instead of recognising the STRAIN ITSELF as the primary invention.

Affected cases: novel Lactobacillus cultures for plant-based dairy, modified Streptococcus strains
with phage resistance, Lactococcus strains for fermentation of plant-based preparations, lactic
bacterium with modified galactokinase expression for texturising, novel Carnobacterium strains,
novel fungal biomass strains (F).

Fix: Added explicit guidance in both PB and F Strain development definitions:
"The key marker is that the STRAIN OR CULTURE ITSELF is the primary innovation — its genetic
makeup, functional properties, or selection from a population — regardless of its downstream
application." Added preamble examples making clear that a novel strain → Strain development
even if its downstream effect is ingredient modification, texturisation, or contaminant control.

---

2. BIOPROCESS DESIGN UNDERDETECTED — F

Second largest error class in F (67% primary correct). The LLM assigned Ingredient optimisation
or Target molecule selection to patents that primarily describe a PRODUCTION METHOD or PROCESS.

Affected cases: casein production method via transgenic microorganisms (→ Bioprocess design,
not Target molecule selection), solid-state fermentation method using brewery spent grain (→
Bioprocess design, not Feedstocks), BSG medium production + fungal biomass (→ Bioprocess
design, not Feedstocks), co-culture method for protein production (→ Bioprocess design), heat/
pipe treatment method to increase casein ratio (→ Bioprocess design), mycelial growth on
substrate as scaffolding process (→ Bioprocess design, since Scaffolding is not a separate
F category).

Root cause: The Bioprocess design definition was narrowly framed around bioreactor design and
did not clearly cover general production process protocols.

Fix: Broadened Bioprocess design in F to explicitly include "novel process protocols — such as
solid-state fermentation methods, co-culture strategies, sequential fermentation protocols, and
production pipeline steps — where the primary innovation is the METHOD or PROCESS of production
rather than the properties of the resulting ingredient." Added decision rule: "Assign Bioprocess
design when the primary novelty is HOW the fermentation or production is conducted, even when
the output is a specific molecule or ingredient." Added preamble example distinguishing Target
molecule selection (molecule is the novelty) vs Bioprocess design (method is the novelty). Added
note that mycelial structuring processes → Bioprocess design.

3. OTHER ASSIGNED WHEN SPECIFIC LABEL APPLIES — CM and general

In CM, two patents about animal feed additives containing cell-growth-relevant compounds were
classified as Other because the primary stated context was farming, not cell culture. Viola's
guidance: "if any of the specific labels apply, always prefer them over the 'Other' category."

Fix: Added "Never assign Other when a specific category clearly applies. Other is a last resort"
to the preamble of all four prompts. Added to CM Cell culture media definition: "Includes
identification of novel sources of growth factors or media components from any origin where the
application is cell culture — even when the patent's primary stated context is a different
application (e.g. animal feed additives)." Added preamble example for CM.

---

4. INGREDIENT OPTIMISATION vs END PRODUCT FORMULATION — PB, CC, F (recurring boundary confusion)

Multiple cases where LLM assigned the wrong category between these two adjacent categories.
Observed both directions of error (EPF given as IO, IO given as EPF).

Key patterns:
- A specific novel COMPOUND (emulsifier, colorant, flavour modifier, enzyme, fatty acid amide)
  for use in AP products → Ingredient optimisation (the compound is the invention, even if
  described being used in a final product)
- A FINISHED PRODUCT FORMULA (specific multi-ingredient composition) → End product formulation
- Agreed corrections: several cases where LLM's EPF or IO was actually correct and Viola updated
  the training label (karitene emulsifier in dairy analogues, prevention of aggregation in nut
  milk, pea flavour composition, IMPROVEMENTS IN OR RELATING TO ORGANIC COMPOUNDS colorant)

Fix: Added preamble examples in PB and CC explicitly illustrating the compound-vs-product
distinction. Added to CC Ingredient optimisation definition: "Also includes specific compounds
(emulsifiers, colorants, flavour modifiers, enzymes) developed or identified for use in AP
products, where the specific compound is the primary object of the invention."

5. SCAFFOLDING vs BIOPROCESS DESIGN (CM, CC)

A cell culture construct comprising fibres for cell adhesion within a bioreactor module was
classified as Scaffolding by the LLM; Viola classified it as Bioprocess design because it is
part of an integrated bioreactor system, not a standalone scaffold material.

Fix: Added to CM and CC Scaffolding definitions: "The primary subject must be the scaffold
MATERIAL or ARCHITECTURE itself, not a production system or bioreactor module that incorporates
scaffolding as one component." Added to CM Bioprocess design: "A scaffold or bioprinting element
that is integrated within a bioreactor or production system design should be classified as
Bioprocess design rather than Scaffolding." Added preamble example in CC.

---

6. TEXTURIZATION METHODS vs SCAFFOLDING for tissue mincing (CC)

A device for mechanically mincing or fragmenting biological tissue into microtissue fragments was
classified as Scaffolding by the LLM. Viola: "Important here is the last sentence — generating
minced tissue → texturisation."

Fix: Added to CC Texturization methods: "Also includes mechanical devices for mincing, cutting,
or fragmenting meat-analogue tissue to produce desired textures." Added preamble example.

---

7. END PRODUCT FORMULATION for slicing/portioning apparatus (CC)

Two patents for slicing machines and methods were classified as Other by the LLM. Viola:
"Patent is about handling the end product." These should be End product formulation.

Fix: Added to CC End product formulation definition: "Also includes patents for equipment and
methods for portioning, slicing, cutting, or forming the final alternative protein product into
its end-use format." Added preamble example.

---

8. BIOPROCESS DESIGN vs TEXTURIZATION for production apparatus (CM, CC)

An extruder system for forming folded extrudates (CM, given: Texturization) was corrected by
Viola to Bioprocess design: "the patent concerns the apparatus and method." Similarly, a
supervisory AI control system for wet extrusion of meat substitutes (CC, given: End product
formulation) was corrected to Bioprocess design.

Fix: Added monitoring/control systems and processing apparatus to CM Bioprocess design definition.
Added AI/machine intelligence control system example to CC Bioprocess design definition and
preamble.

---

9. CROP DEVELOPMENT vs INGREDIENT OPTIMISATION (PB)

A patent for pea varieties with improved taste profile (given: Ingredient optimisation) was
classified as Crop development by the LLM. Viola: "it mainly concerns INGREDIENTS FROM the crop
but not the development of the crop line itself."

Fix: Added to PB Crop development definition: "When a patent focuses on the ingredients or
functional properties derived from a crop rather than the plant genetics, breeding, or variety
itself, prefer Ingredient optimisation."

---

10. CELL LINE DEVELOPMENT vs END PRODUCT FORMULATION (CM)

Two identical patents (Food Products Comprising Avian Stem Cells) were classified as Cell line
development by the LLM; Viola: "the focus is the product which consists of specific cell line
and cell culture media but they are not the focus of the patent → End product formulation."

Fix: Added to CM Cell line development definition: "Does not include food products that
incorporate cells as an ingredient without primary innovation in the cell line itself, which
belongs in End product formulation." Added preamble example.

---

11. TEXTURIZATION METHODS for texture-function ingredients (F)

A patent for a microalgae protein extract with specific texturising/coagulation function was
classified as Ingredient optimisation. Viola: "the ingredient described here has the clear focus
of improving texture of products, so texturisation methods is the right label."

Fix: Added to F Texturization methods: "Also includes ingredients specifically extracted or
processed where the primary stated function is texturization (e.g. a protein extract for
coagulation or gelling) — prefer Texturization methods over Ingredient optimisation when
texturizing function is the primary purpose of the ingredient." Added preamble example.
